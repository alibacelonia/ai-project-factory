# Dark Luxe

Use deep matte surfaces, restrained metallic accents, subtle lighting, and refined typography for premium tools and services.

- **Palette:** Default dark background `#090A0D`, foreground `#F5F2EA`, primary `#D6B46A`, secondary `#A8A29A`, accent `#7DD3FC`, surface `#15171C`, border `#343740`, focus `#F5D98D`. Light alternative background `#F8F6F1`, foreground `#171717`, surface `#FFFFFF`, primary `#80621E`, focus `#5E450E`.
- **Type:** Elegant display serif or high-contrast sans for headings paired with a neutral sans body. Use accents sparingly; forms never use fragile thin weights.
- **Surfaces:** 10–16px radii, dark tonal layers, hairline warm borders, controlled radial highlights, and very soft shadows. Avoid noisy faux-metal gradients.
- **Components:** Understated buttons with one gold primary, dark inputs, quiet cards, refined dialogs, compact premium badges, and high-clarity tables.
- **Interaction:** Hover brightens an edge or accent; active lowers elevation. Focus ring is warm and unmistakable. Disabled states remain readable instead of disappearing into black.
- **Responsive:** Preserve breathing room and simplify highlight layers; premium should not mean oversized mobile gutters.
- **Accessibility:** Test warm-on-dark text, especially small labels. Do not use dim gray for primary content or gold alone to indicate selection.
- **Theme/performance:** Dark is preferred, but light is intentional and fully readable. Use static CSS lighting, optimized images, limited blur, no ambient animation by default, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.

Representative preview: premium developer workspace with refined navigation, analytics cards, plan badge, dark form, and crisp data table.
