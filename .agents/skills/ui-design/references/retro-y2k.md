# Retro / Y2K

Use optimistic early-digital color, glossy controls, pixel accents, chrome-like details, and playful window framing in a modern semantic interface.

- **Palette:** Light background `#DDFBFF`, foreground `#24134F`, primary `#6B4EFF`, secondary `#FF66C4`, accent `#00B8A9`, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#7457A8`, focus `#003DFF`. Dark background `#190F2E`, foreground `#FFF8FF`, surface `#2B1946`, primary `#B8A5FF`, accent `#59F0D7`.
- **Type:** Pixel or techno face only for short display accents, paired with a clean sans for body and controls. Preserve normal casing and readable spacing.
- **Surfaces:** Glossy gradients, 8–16px radius, chrome/pixel trim, floating windows, and selective hard highlights. Avoid texture behind reading content.
- **Components:** Window cards, glossy primary buttons, bordered inputs, tabs, playful badges, and familiar tables/dialogs. Icons must be crisp and labeled when ambiguous.
- **Interaction:** Hover brightens gloss or adds a pixel marker; press reduces highlight. Focus is a modern solid ring; disabled controls lose gloss but keep readable text. Avoid cursor trails and sound unless explicitly requested and controllable.
- **Responsive:** Flatten multi-window arrangements, retain one nostalgic accent per section, and prevent pixel decorations from consuming touch space.
- **Accessibility:** Novelty styling never replaces labels, states, or semantic structure. Maintain contrast on gradients and supply reduced-animation alternatives.
- **Theme/performance:** Dark mode becomes arcade-like but restrains glow. Prefer small CSS gradients and SVG/pixel assets, optimize media, avoid continuous sparkle, and honor reduced motion.
